### About me

I'm passionate about turning ideas into thoughtfully built tools.

By day, I work as a **Cloud Engineer**, focusing on cloud infrastructure, security, automation, and making systems more reliable. Outside of work, you'll usually find me on side projects that scratch a personal itch — building tools for myself or tinkering with new tech, often inspired by a problem I want to solve or a skill I want to learn.

Beyond engineering, I care a lot about **community** and creating opportunities for people to learn and connect. Through groups like **AWS User Group Davao**, **DurianPy**, and **Google Developer Group Davao**, I help organize events and contribute to the growth of the local tech ecosystem in Mindanao.

I believe technology is at its best when it helps people create, connect, and solve meaningful problems — whether I'm designing an experience, developing a project, architecting infrastructure, or contributing to a community initiative.
